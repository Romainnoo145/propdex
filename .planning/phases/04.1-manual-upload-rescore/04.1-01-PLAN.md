<objective>
Wire `rescore_parcels_for_document` ARQ enqueue into two places:
1. The manual admin upload route (`policy_documents/router.py`) so every woonvisie upload triggers cascade re-scoring.
2. The opportunistic probe orchestrator (`data_source_probe_orchestrator.py`) so pre-existing documents that flip `missing → live` during probe-on-GET also enqueue re-scoring.

Both wire-points are gated by a single `CASCADE_RESCORE_SOURCE_KEYS: frozenset[str]` constant declared in `rescore_cascade_job.py`. M050+ extractors land by adding their source_key to the frozenset — no other wire changes needed.

Purpose: Close INT-04 gap where admin-uploaded woonvisie docs are reflected in the data-bronnen hub UI (status='live') but do NOT propagate to `scan_results` until the weekly cron runs. After this plan, every manual upload is on parity with the weekly auto-ingest cron.

Output: ~50 LoC production changes across 3 files + 6 integration tests in 1 new test file.
</objective>
